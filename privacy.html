Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Glimmer Glam ("the app") is designed for children and respects their privacy. We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information.

What the app stores

Your creations, star count and settings are saved only on your own device, and are removed when you delete the app. We cannot see them. If you use iCloud device backup, Apple may back this data up under your Apple account.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups.
  • No advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics tools.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No collection of names, emails, locations, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive payment details. Purchases are protected by a parental gate inside the app.

Sharing

If a grown-up chooses to share a picture of a look, it is sent only to the place they choose using the standard iOS share sheet. The app does not upload it anywhere itself.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, the app complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR-K and similar laws. Parents with questions can contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
