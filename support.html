Support

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the ⚙️ button, answer the grown-ups-only question, then tap "Restore Purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How does the free trial work?

Everything is unlocked for 3 days after installing. Afterwards a good set of items stays free, and a single one-time purchase unlocks the rest.

Where are my saved looks?

Tap 📚 in the top corner. Looks are stored on your device only; deleting the app removes them.
